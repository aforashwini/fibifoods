01 — Overview

Why this strategy, why now

FibiFoods sits at an inflection point: strong product–market fit, a loyal organic community, and an untapped paid channel. This document converts that potential into a systematic, profitable growth engine.

1

Full-funnel coverage

Awareness, consideration, conversion, and retention each have dedicated spend and creative. No revenue leaks through gaps.

2

First-party data first

Email lists, order data, and on-site behaviour drive audience creation, insulating performance against privacy changes.

3

Creative as growth lever

Weekly creative testing with structured win/loss analysis. Winning assets scale; losers are killed within 14 days.

4

Profitability guardrails

Every campaign has a hard CAC ceiling and minimum ROAS threshold. Budget shifts automatically to channels that clear the bar; underperformers are paused, not tolerated.

Strategic north star: Achieve a blended ROAS of 4.2× within 90 days while holding new-customer CAC at or below £18, then scale budget 20 % month-on-month without degrading efficiency.

Profit = Revenue − COGS − AdSpend − Fulfilment

Ad spend is optimised against gross margin, not top-line revenue.

02 — Audience

Who we are reaching

Four high-value personas, each with distinct motivations and media habits, form the targeting architecture across all paid channels.

A

The Conscious Nourisher

Primary · 35 % of spend

Urban professional, 28–42. Buys organic, reads labels, cooks frequently. Engages on Instagram and Pinterest. High LTV; price-tolerant for quality.

B

The Busy Parent

Primary · 30 % of spend

Household decision-maker, 32–48. Values convenience without compromising on ingredients. Heavy Facebook and YouTube user; responds to recipe and time-saving angles.

C

The Fitness Optimiser

Secondary · 20 % of spend

Active lifestyle, 22–38. Tracks macros, follows fitness creators, shops on mobile. Responds strongly to protein and clean-label messaging. TikTok and Instagram primary.

D

The Gift Giver

Seasonal · 15 % of spend

Buying for others, any age. High AOV, low repeat frequency. Activates in Q4, Mother's Day, and Valentine's. Google Search and Pinterest key; gifting creative essential.

Lookalike strategy: Seed all four personas from verified purchaser lists (min. 1,000 matched records) before launching cold prospecting. Lookalike audiences consistently outperform interest-only targeting by 30–50 % on CPL in the food category.

06 — Roadmap

90-day launch roadmap

Three sequential phases, each with a clear objective and exit criteria before advancing.

Weeks 1–3

Phase 1 — Foundation

Install and validate all tracking infrastructure before spending a pound on media.

  • GA4 + server-side Meta Pixel + Google Tag Manager deploy
  • Conversion events mapped and verified (purchase, add-to-cart, checkout start)
  • Product feed audit — titles, descriptions, categories, GTINs
  • Audience seed lists uploaded (email → custom audiences)
  • Creative brief issued; first batch of assets produced
Weeks 4–8

Phase 2 — Learning

Launch campaigns in learning mode. Accumulate signal, not scale.

  • Meta: Advantage+ Shopping + 2 prospecting ad sets live
  • Google: Branded search, non-branded purchase intent, Shopping campaigns
  • TikTok: 2 In-Feed Video campaigns with 3 creative variants each
  • Weekly creative review; pause underperformers after 2,000 impressions
  • Exit criterion: ≥ 50 conversions per campaign before scaling
Weeks 9–13

Phase 3 — Scale

Winning campaigns receive budget step-ups. Pinterest and YouTube enter the mix. Monthly reallocation cadence begins.

  • Budget +20 % on campaigns beating ROAS target
  • Pinterest Shopping Ads and YouTube pre-roll launched
  • Lookalike audiences expanded (2 %, 5 % tiers) from converter seed
  • Retargeting pools activated on Display and Meta DPA
  • Monthly performance review and budget reallocation documented
