About Fibi

Fiber Shouldn't Be Complicated

Most Americans get only half the fiber they need each day. We started Fibi Foods because we believe closing that gap shouldn't mean choking down a chalky supplement or decoding a 30-ingredient label.

Every Fibi bar is built around a short list of whole, plant-based foods — oats, dates, nuts, seeds, and real fruit. That's it. We use chicory root fiber and psyllium husk to hit 12 grams per bar, so you get nearly half your daily value in one delicious snack. No protein isolates, no sugar alcohols, no mystery gums.

Our Story

Born in a Kitchen, Built on a Mission

Fibi started in 2023 when our founder — a nutritionist and lifelong snack skeptic — couldn't find a single fiber bar that didn't taste like cardboard or come loaded with ingredients she'd never recommend to a client.

So she made her own. The first batch sold out at a local farmers' market in under an hour. Neighbors started placing weekly orders. The rest, as they say, is fiber history.

Today, Fibi Foods is still made in small batches with the same real-food philosophy: if it doesn't belong in your body, it doesn't belong in our bars.

"I wanted to make the bar I wished existed — something I'd actually look forward to eating, not just tolerate for the fiber."

— Ashwini, Founder of Fibi Foods
